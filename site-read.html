The first engagement

Site Read

Switch to Urdu / اردو میں پڑھیں →

Before anything gets built, someone has to look at what is actually there. A Site Read is that engagement. Manar comes to the operation, examines the data and the systems already running, and produces a written assessment of what is possible.

What happens

A visit to the site, not a meeting in an office. The cameras that are actually installed, the records as they are actually kept, the machines as they actually run. Most organisations describe their data as more organised than it turns out to be, which is not a criticism. It is simply what happens when nobody has had reason to audit it before.

What you receive

A written assessment covering what data the operation currently produces and in what condition, which of it is usable for training as it stands, which registered model fits the work or whether none of them do, what hardware that model would require, what the deployment would involve in sequence, and where the risks are.

What it is not

Not a proposal. Not a sales visit. Not a commitment to deploy anything.

The part most vendors leave out

A Site Read can conclude that this is not worth doing. If the data is not there, or the problem is better solved without AI, or the cost does not justify the return, the assessment says so in writing. That outcome is not a failed engagement. It is the assessment doing its job, and it is cheaper to learn now than after building something.
